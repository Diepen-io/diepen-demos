# Direction

Written from `docs/brief.md`, the offer (`ai-consulting/offer.md`) and the demo list (`ai-consulting/demos/demos.md`), which Leander wrote.

## Who it is for

Owners of small service businesses with 1 to 20 people where one task eats hours every week: inquiries, documents, follow-ups after calls.
They see the demos on diepen.ai, in a LinkedIn video, or live on an Audit Review call in their own brand.

## The problem they have today

They cannot picture what "an AI automation" would do in their business, and Leander has no client case study to show them yet.

## What success looks like in three months

All three demos are live on their own URLs, each with a case study page on diepen.ai, a LinkedIn video and a working "Make it yours" for sales calls.
At least one Automation Sprint was sold with a demo in the call.

## What we do not build

- Real integrations with Gmail, Instagram, Drive, a CRM or a calendar. Every channel is a simulation.
- Accounts, sign-in, or any storage of a visitor's data on a server.
- Paid transcription or any AI provider other than Claude.
- Client results. The demos say they are demos.

## Current focus

Demo 1, Inbox autopilot, then Document chaser, then Call to close.
